FAQ

Common questions

Is Price Action Trainer free?
Yes. The core tool is completely free with no sign-up required. Creating a free account lets you save your session progress across devices so you can pick up where you left off.
What is price action trading?
Price action trading is a methodology that makes trading decisions based solely on the movement of price, without relying on lagging indicators like moving averages or RSI. Practitioners read raw candlestick patterns, support and resistance levels, market structure (higher highs/lows), and momentum to anticipate the next likely move.
How is this different from paper trading?
Paper trading in real time means waiting for bars to form — that's 6.5 hours a day for a daily trader. Price Action Trainer lets you practice hundreds of setups in an hour by replaying historical data. The prediction system also forces you to commit before seeing the outcome, which builds genuine skill rather than hindsight bias.
What assets can I practice on?
The dataset library covers US stocks (S&P 500 components), sector ETFs, forex pairs (9 majors and crosses), cryptocurrencies (BTC, ETH, SOL, and others), gold, silver, crude oil, and major global indices including the FTSE, DAX, and Nikkei.
What timeframes are available?
Daily, weekly, and monthly candles are available, with historical ranges going back to 2000 or earlier for major instruments. This lets you practice reading both intraday structure patterns and macro trend analysis.
What is blind mode?
Blind mode hides the ticker symbol so you can't use prior knowledge about a stock's history to bias your read. You're forced to read the pure price structure — which is exactly what you'd do trading an unfamiliar instrument in live markets.
What does noise injection do?
Noise adds small random perturbations to each bar's OHLC values. This prevents you from recognising specific historical price sequences you've already studied, keeping every session novel even on datasets you've used before.
