Why it works

Passive watching builds
hindsight, not skill.

When the outcome is already on the chart, every move looks obvious. You're not building pattern recognition — you're building the ability to rationalise moves after they happen. That's useless in real time.

Real-time paper trading is better, but a daily chart gives you one bar per day. To get 200 meaningful reps you'd need eight months. Your feedback loop is almost non-existent.

The trainer compresses time. Step through history bar by bar, forced to commit before the reveal. Every press of → is a judgment — right or wrong, permanently recorded. That's the signal your brain needs.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. The trainer runs in your browser with no sign-up required. Creating an account saves session progress across devices — predictions, trades, notes, and your position in the dataset. The leaderboard requires an account to participate.
How is this different from paper trading?
Paper trading in real time means waiting for bars to form. On a daily chart that's 6.5 hours per bar. Historical replay gives you 200+ reps per session. More importantly, the prediction system forces you to commit before the outcome — eliminating the hindsight bias that makes passive watching almost useless for building skill.
What is blind mode?
Blind mode hides the ticker symbol. You can't lean on prior knowledge about a stock's history. You're forced to read the structure, not the name — which is the actual skill.
What does noise injection do?
It adds small random perturbations to each bar's OHLC values. This prevents recognising specific historical sequences you've already studied, keeping every session novel even on datasets you've used before.
Can I review my trades after a session?
Yes. The session summary shows entry, TP, SL, exit price, R:R, and outcome for every mapped trade. Click Review on any trade and the chart jumps back to your entry bar — you step forward bar by bar and see exactly how the move played out against your levels.
How will the leaderboard work?
The weekly leaderboard will rank users by directional accuracy across sessions with 30 or more predictions — to prevent gaming with a single lucky session. Streak, total sessions, and best R:R will also be tracked. The board resets at midnight UTC each Monday. It requires an account so your sessions can be attributed.
What is price action trading?
Price action trading means making decisions based solely on how price moves — without lagging indicators like RSI or MACD. The discipline involves reading candlestick patterns, market structure (higher highs and lows, support and resistance), and the behaviour of wicks at key levels.
